Coursework Description   
Alumni Influencers - Web Client 
Background Scenario 
Part 2: University Analytics & Intelligence Dashboard 
Weighting: 60%  
Prerequisites: Successful completion of Part 1 
 
Background & Business Context 
 
Universities traditionally struggle with understanding what happens to graduates after they 
leave. Current methods rely on: 
●​
Annual graduate surveys with 30-40% response rates 
●​
Self-reported data collected 6-12 months after graduation 
●​
Historical information that's already outdated when analysed 
●​
Limited insight into career progression beyond first destination 
●​
No visibility into post-graduation professional development 
This creates a critical gap: by the time universities identify skills gaps or emerging industry 
requirements, they're already 2-3 years behind. Graduates discover these gaps themselves 
and independently acquire certifications, attend bootcamps, and complete professional 
courses to remain competitive. 
The Alumni Influencers Solution 
Part 1 created a platform where alumni maintain detailed, current professional profiles to 
compete for visibility. This generates a living, real-time dataset of actual graduate outcomes. 
Part 2 builds the University Analytics Dashboard - a dedicated client application that 
transforms this alumni data into actionable intelligence for curriculum development and 
strategic planning. 
Real-World Intelligence Examples:  
 
Scenario 1: Skills Gap Detection The University Computer Science programme doesn't 
include Docker/Kubernetes in the curriculum. Within 18 months of graduation, 73% of CS 
alumni have independently acquired container orchestration certifications. This signals a 
critical curriculum gap that the university can address before the next cohort graduates 
unprepared. 
Scenario 2: Emerging Career Pathways Data shows 23% of Business Management 
graduates now work in "Data Analytics" roles despite no formal data science training. Alumni 
have independently completed Python, SQL, and Tableau courses. This reveals an 
emerging career pathway the university isn't preparing students for. 
Scenario 3: Industry Demand Tracking Over the past 6 months, cloud certifications (AWS, 
Azure, GCP) have increased 156% among graduates. The university can proactively 
integrate cloud computing earlier in the curriculum rather than reactively adding it years later. 
Scenario 4: Professional Development Trends The most completed post-graduation courses 
are Agile/Scrum certifications (31% of graduates), suggesting universities should integrate 
agile methodologies into project modules rather than leaving graduates to learn these critical 
workplace practices independently. 
 
Part 2 Features Overview 
Core Features You Will Build 
1.​ Registration & Authentication  
o​ Email-based registration (university domain required) 
o​ Email verification system 
o​ Secure login/logout with sessions 
o​ Password reset functionality 
2.​ Complete Webpages 
o​ Dashboard 
o​ Graphs Pages 
o​ View Alumni by programme, graduation date and industry sector. 
3.​ Blind Bidding System  
o​ Place bids without seeing highest bid amount 
o​ Receive win/lose status feedback 
o​ Update bids (increase only) 
o​ Monthly limit enforcement (3 features per month) 
o​ Automated midnight winner selection (Mark the profile that will be displayed) 
4.​ Security to restrict client Access through bearer tokens  
o​ Decide how different clients will access the data 
o​ View usage statistics i.e. number of times and time stamps of clients logging 
in and usage of keys / tokens and endpoints accessed  
o​ API key Scoping: API keys should be scoped to specific client applications. 
For example, the university analytics dashboard should only have 
permissions like ["read:alumni", "read:analytics"], while the mobile AR app 
should have its own separate key with permissions like 
["read:alumni_of_day"]. This ensures that a compromised analytics 
dashboard key cannot be used to access mobile AR endpoints, and vice 
versa.
